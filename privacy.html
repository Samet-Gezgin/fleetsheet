Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

In short: FleetSheet does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and makes no internet connections.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the FleetSheet app for iPhone ("the app"). It explains what happens to the information you enter into the app.

Information you enter

You can type details about yachts into the app, such as specifications, crew numbers, rates, routes, notes, and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of captains, agencies and owners.

  • This information is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage.
  • The app also keeps an automatic backup file of your entries, FleetSheet-backup.json, in its Documents folder on your iPhone. You can see it in the Files app under "On My iPhone › FleetSheet".
  • The developer never receives, sees or has access to anything you enter.

If you enter contact details of other people, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep them, in the same way as keeping them in a notebook or your phone's contacts.

Business card scanning: camera and contacts

FleetSheet can scan a business card and turn it into a new contact. This feature uses two iPhone permissions, which the app asks for the first time you use it:

  • Camera: used only while you are scanning a card. The text on the card is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition. The card image is not saved by the app and is never sent anywhere.
  • Contacts: used only to save the scanned card as a new contact. The app shows you the filled-in contact first, and nothing is saved unless you tap Done. The contact's note includes the name of the yacht you scanned it from and the date. The app does not read, change, upload or share your existing contacts.

You can turn either permission off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. The rest of FleetSheet keeps working without them.

Information the app collects

None. FleetSheet does not collect usage data, diagnostics, location, device identifiers or any other information. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party software development kits.

Internet connections

FleetSheet works entirely offline and does not connect to the internet. Nothing you enter or scan is sent to the developer or to any server.

Exporting your data

When you tap "Export CSV", the app creates a spreadsheet file of your entries and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where that file goes, for example the Files app, email or a messaging app. From that point the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Contacts you saved from scanned cards are part of your iPhone contacts and follow your own contacts and iCloud settings. Those backups and settings are controlled by you and by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any yacht inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data and its backup file from the device. Contacts you saved from scanned cards stay in your Contacts app until you delete them there. Copies you exported earlier are not affected.

Children

FleetSheet is a business tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
